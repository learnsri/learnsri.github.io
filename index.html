About The Event

Fostering Secure Digital Innovation

LEARN37 is part of the Lanka Education And Research Network (LEARN), bringing together representatives from Sri Lanka's education, research, and technology communities.

This year's programme will place a special focus on cybersecurity in education and research, providing a platform to discuss emerging cyber threats, institutional preparedness, incident response, practical solutions, and collaboration.

The programme will also include the LEARN Annual General Meeting (AGM) for official representatives of LEARN member institutions.
