Discover LEARN37

Safeguarding Academic Futures

LEARN37 represents the benchmark summit hosted by the Lanka Education And Research Network, converging university vice-chancellors, IT directors, security architects, and academic researchers.

This edition spotlights an urgent national imperative: Strengthening Cybersecurity in Education & Research. As university assets face sophisticated targeted ransomware, phishing, and exfiltration attempts, LEARN37 provides the actionable blueprints, institutional frameworks, and collective incident response capabilities needed to defend our academic digital space.

Concurrently, the closed-door 37th LEARN Meeting convenes the designated institutional representatives to shape connectivity roadmaps, digital public goods, and shared cloud platforms for the upcoming decade.
