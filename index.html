About The Event

LEARN37 is part of the Lanka Education And Research Network (LEARN), bringing together representatives from Sri Lanka's education, research, and technology communities.

This year's programme will place a special focus on cybersecurity in education and research, providing an essential platform to discuss emerging cyber threats, institutional preparedness, incident response, practical solutions, and national collaboration.

The programme will also include the official LEARN Annual General Meeting (AGM) for official representatives of LEARN member institutions.
